Late 2025 Update

Written: 2025-09-05

Boston Concert

As the end of 2024 was coming to a close, I wanted to do something fun before it ended. I wasn't too sure what to do, luckily one day one of my URI friends messages me about how they recently got a car and wanted to visit. We met up around where I lived, he brought his friend who I also knew and we causually walked around the park and a local festival. In conversation I brought up the games I was recently playing, we went back and forth for a bit until one of them mentions how there was a concert coming up in Boston around October for NieR.

My eyes lit up, I'd seen video game concerts online before and always wanted to see one in person; but never had the opportunity to before. After we parted ways I immediately got on my computer and booked tickets for it. I messaged them that I booked my ticket and soon after they did theirs. After some time, the concert's date came up and I went into my work office and when I was done for the day, I started my drive into Boston.

I knew there was going to be traffic but, wow. I was theoretically only 1 hour from Boston from work but it took 3 hours to get there. I guess a Friday at 5pm was prime time for deadlock traffic. First you get past Providence traffic and then the Boston traffic. Eventually I get into the city itself and prepare to park in the lot I reserved. Well, apparently they overbooked it and I had to drive loops in order to find a spot, spending another hour of time just getting to the point where I'm officially there.

My friends and I planned to eat before going into the concert, but it was starting in half an hour by the time I even got out of my car, so we decided to eat after. I sighed in reflief when I finally saw them in the waiting hall. I was particularlly excited since I had been a NieR fan for some time but they only recently that year started NieR Automata. For them it was going to be a little confusing following along, but still fun nonetheless.

As expected there was some cosplayers walking around and all kinds of outfits. One guy had a full Emile helmet and staff, he could barely even sit in his chair he was so fitted out. I got a few pictures and went to my seat before the concert started. Eventually the lighted dimmed and I got to enjoy the full orchestra in person for the first time. It was very enjoyable, I had pretty good front row seats and paid attention to the players since I'd never seen any of this in person. As the concert came to a close one of the producers came out to thank the viewers, my friends and I from URI were smuggly happy that we could understand him without the translator.

After the concert we went back to the hall and picked up some souvenirs. My friend got me a nice music box from the game and we got some food before heading our ways. It was already almost midnight by the end of the concert so when I got back at 3AM I flopped on my bed and fell right asleep.
