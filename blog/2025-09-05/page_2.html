Late 2025 Update

Written: 2025-09-05

Life Events

Family Death

Not to bring the mood down, but right after I came back from Egypt I was told that my step-father was hospitalized. Me and my brother already knew something was going on while abroad since we were getting texts, but from what I remember it had something to do with low blood sugar and possibily drinking. Either way once I got back and a week had passed I was told from my grandmother that my step-father passed away. Some part of me knew I was going to get that message, but it was still strange to hear in person.

The first day after I had heard the news I didn't really now how to process it, like it wasn't real. This wasn't the first time someone in my family passed away, but it was the first time it was someone so young. When my grandfather passed away in 2019 it was sad but not unexpected. He was already dealing with parkinsons, alzeimers, and so much more a few years before his death. I was in college when it all happened, so I saw the change in personality, the hallucinations, and the sort. I'm lucky that I got to say my final goodbyes before he passed since I know not everyone gets to do that.

My grandfather though was in his 70s; my step-father was only in his late 40s. I knew he wasn't in great shape being an alcholic diabetic but it's always a shock to hear of a death. Another week or so passed and I was told of the wake and funeral dates. I didn't know what to expect really, I didn't know much of his family and the fact I'd never been to a funeral before. When my grandfather passed I didn't get to go to the funeral since I had 2 exams and a presentation at school when they did it.

I arrived and saw a closed casket with a picture of my step-father from high school on it. I was still somewhat in shock, seeing the casket there, knowing someone I only saw a year ago was in there had me in tears. When I was there I got to meet his aunts and uncles, saw his brother (who I hadn't seen since I was a little kid), and his mother. My brother, cousin, and I sat there in the front row talking for a bit until we felt it was time to go and we went back home.

The next day we went to the funeral and my brother gave his speech on our step-father. They played Frank Sinatra while lowering his casket and I cried next to my brother and cousin. Once they laid him down next to his father's grave we sat in silence until everyone started to leave for the after party. We went to the after party and hung around for a bit, but eventually we left. Before heading straight home my brother wanted to take the time to go by all the houses we lived at and went to as kids, since he flew out for the funeral and hasn't been back home for a while.

We first went by my step-father's house, then the first apartment my mom and step-father lived at, my grandfather's old house, my mom and step-father's first house, their second house, and eventually back home. We get back home and heard that our uncle is hosting a party since a lot of people from my side of the family came down for the funeral.

We went to the party and most everyone went into the basement to play music together. I stayed up stairs with one of the dogs and processed what just happened over the last week. Grieving over my step-father's death felt different than my grandfather. With my grandfather it felt easier since I only had good memories of him and it felt somewhat of a relief that he was no longer in pain when it finally ended. With my step-father though, it felt complicated.

I saw the good in him as a kid and how much he tried to be a good step-parent, but I also have a lot of negative experiences and anxities from childhood. I was trying to reconcile all of the good and bad in my head, trying to figure out what to do with these emotions.

I came to the conclusion that everything was in the past and was already settled; any worries I had about him went away. I could work on the past trauma inflicted with a professional and feel like that chapter in my life has come to a close.

I love my step-father, he introduced to me so many things; including my favorite video game series of all time (The Legend of Zelda). He'd play games with me on the weekened, play catch at the beach, go on the boat in the ocean, go on trails, and so on. I didn't have to dwell on all the bad things he did so long ago.

As time went on I slowly processed these emotions and felt a sense of conclusion on the matter. It happended, it's done, and it's over. I started to get back up to speed at work and getting my life back on track again.
