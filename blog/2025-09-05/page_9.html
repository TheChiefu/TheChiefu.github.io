Late 2025 Update

Written: 2025-09-05

Summer

Upcoming Summer

As summer was approaching I was trying to figure out what to do with the time. Do I do a summer semester at school to speed up my degree? Do I try to study some math that I need for a future class? Do I take it off to relax?

After some time and thinking it over I decided that I was going to relearn math. I opened up Khan Academy after years of not touching and and decided that I'm going from start to finish. Somewhat as a joke, I started with Kindergarden math and worked my way up until 4th grade in about a week. As some time passed I felt like it was a waste of time and went to more specific lessons like Linear Algebra in order to prepare for some of my more math heavy classes.

I was keeping a good streak going for a while doing my lessons and practicing equations. But eventually I started to falter and felt like I was going to get more out of my summer by taking a class over the summer. I bought a couple of textbooks for math to look at in my free time and some for upcoming classes for pre-review.

Once summer registration came up I signed up for Computer Animation since I was interested in learning how animation worked from a coding perspective.

Computer Animation work with my cat Taz distracting me

During Summer

Summer finally hit and I got ready for my new class. I read the required syllbus and excitely looked up some math videos to get more used to vectors, the dot product, and so on. Around this time one of my friends who I hadn't seen in around 3 years reached out to one of our mutual friends to meet up. Without specifing much detail he was having some relationship issues with his partner. He met up with me and 2 others, catching up on the time that had passed. At first it was a little awkward considering the topic but I wanted to be there for support and overall it went well.

I got back home and hastily did my school assignments and upcoming project since my friend was planning on going camping in New Hampshire a few weeks later. When we did go we saw some sight seeing spots and camped for a night. I enjoyed my time being out there and catching up, since I often feel like I want to go out and do something but never find the time or place to do it.

When talking he brought up that in a few weeks he was planning on going to New Hampshire. A few years back we planned to go camping together and never ended up doing it. So when he brought it up, I told him that we could both go one weekend. He agreed and not too far after we went sight seeing and camping for a few days.

Profile Lake, New Hampshire

On the day we were planning on going home, we drove around the white mountain area for bit and decided since we were already up north to head east over to Maine. We'd both have never been there, so we took a few hour drive and hung around the Bug Light park for a bit. Eventually around noon or so we decide to split up and drive back home south to RI. I was estatic to go out camping and to new places I hadn't been to, since for a while after high school I always wanted to do primitive camping.

Bug Light Park, Maine

After the trip I fell back to my normal routine of work and school, doing the required lessons for the class such as implementing splines, IK machines, and all the fun stuff. I eventually get a text from my friend I went camping with and we decide we want to go somewhere really remote. We decided on visiting Moose River Forest in Inlet, New York. Throughout the week I bought some camping supplies to be more ready, then on a Friday afternoon we met up and did the long 6 hour drive to New York.

Along the way we stopped at different places and eventually started to get tired of driving. So at some point we divert to a slightly different camp area around the same place we originally decided. However, we get lost and suddenly it's 12AM and we have no idea where the camping area is. Eventually we stop on a side area near the highway and sleep in our cars for the night. Just before going to sleep though we take step outside the car and look up. The stars were faint, but I hadn't seen so many of them in the sky in a really long time. In RI where I live it's hard to find an area without much light pollution. I setup my camera and tried to take long exposure shot to get a bright clear picture, but since I couldn't see that well I didn't notice there was a power line right in the middle of the view.

Either way we go to sleep and the next morning we head over to the area where the primitive campsites where. We get to the road where they sites are and drive for almost an hour deep into woods, leaving GPS signals behind. Eventually getting to a spot we think looks good and start to setup. Quickly realizing it was a bad area since there was an outhouse near the site where flies where everywhere. Not just tiny flies, but large horseflies and very annoying insects that ignored our bug sprays. We pickup and drive slightly deeper into the woods until we get to a relatively good spot. There were still annoying bugs but a lot better than before (it's still the deep woods, so it's expected to deal with insects).

We lucked out, our camping spot was right near a hiking trail that lead to a lake. When we set up our gear and got everything ready we hopped on the trail and walked for about an hour to a large lake. We thought about swimming in it but took a quick look at the water, it had tons of tiny insects and discoloration; so we decided to just sit near it and talk. We talked for so long about anything you could think of, but it was just nice sitting in the middle of deep woods away from absolutely everything. Hearing the trees sway, frogs croaking, and grass moving. No sounds of cars, planes, or anything of the sorts.

After some time we hear other people coming up the trail and decide that we'd been there long enough and wanted to let other people have some time on the spot to themselves. We head back to our camping spot, start up the fire, and making some hotdogs during the night. One thing I did not anticipate since it'd been a while since I went camping was how dark and quiet everything was. Any time we'd stop talking it would be deafeningly slient. No crickets, tree leaves, or anything. It was very eerrie but weirdly relaxing. It was also strange how dark it was, I mean I'd obviously been out at night, but it always seems like in regular civlization there is some dim light somewhere; but out there was nothing. We heard a tree fall not far from us, but other than that, silence.

Once it got around 11 we went to bed; him in his car, me in my tent. I thought about keeping the top of my tent uncovered but it started to slightly drizzle, prompting me to put it on. As the night went by I heard thunder get closer and closer. Some time in the early morning it absolutely downpoured and crackled thunder. Luckly my tent did very well and I only got a tiny 1 inch puddle in the corner of my tent. By the time it was 8 or 9 in the morning, we packed up our stuff and made our way back towards home. We wanted to explore the town a little bit, but knew that it was already Sunday and we had a 6 hour drive ahead of us; so it'd be better to head straight back.

We split up, I did the drive straight-through without stopping and finally got home. Nothing much to eventful happened since that last trip, but we have plans to go to Vermont some time in the future and possibly Canada if we both get some time off. Either way though I go back to my normal work and school routine and wrap up my summer course with a solid "A" grade.
