Work, School, and Life

Written: 2025-09-05

Hospitaliation

In April I was hospitalized for my appendix nearly bursting. For months I was having back pains but I thought it was due to me mostly sitting all day (which probably didn't help). However, towards the end of March I basically never went into the office at work since the pain was so bad. For weeks I'd do my work in bed since I had no energy to stand up or sit.

I felt bad for my classmates since I'd go a week with barely any work done on the project and my work motiviation was slowly dimishing. I kept thinking to myself that I should go to the hospital, but also felt that maybe I was overreacting and my pain wasn't as bad as I thought was. One day though I couldn't get any sleep and was moaning in pain the entire night. Eventually my grandmother asks me if I'm okay. I sit there thinking and eventually tell her, "no, I should probably go to the hospital".

I get dressed, message my manager telling him where I'm going, and get driven to the emergency room. I eventually get in, the pain getting worse, and I end up sitting in the room for a few minutes waiting for a doctor. They do some preliminary searches, get a scan done for me, then after a few hours come back in. They initally told me my appenix burst, but after more inspection tell me that it's inflammed but leaking.

After they told me that it started to make a lot of more sense why I was feeling so much pain. I wasn't having any sharp pain, but a lot of aching/pulsing pain that'd come and go. I was only midly comfortable lying completely flat. Anything else and it was mid-back pain that'd cause me to tire easily.

Once it was confirmed they brought me into another room pre-surgery. They got all the paperwork done and I went in shortly after. Inhaling gas and quickly falling into sleep. I eventually wake up in the bed being moved to my new room. They had a few incisions but it went well, I had a tube on my lower abdomin where they had puss and blood drain to get rid of anything I had left in my body.

Me at the hospital after surgery

It was tough the 4-5 days I was there. I had a rush of emotions thinking about what could of happened if I didn't go to the hospital ("Would I have died?", I thought). The bordem and monotony of sitting there all day and the worry of work and school in my mind. Eventually they discharge me and I'm mostly feeling better. This quickly goes away once I start taking my antibiotics.

Any time I'd eat something I'd start to violently vomit and have diahera. The first day or two I tried to put up with it, since I thought I had to take all my medicine in order to get better. But as time went on, I couldn't sleep, and was throwing up more than I ate. I went back into the ER and stayed another 4 days until they got me through my antibiotics, making sure I had an IV constantly on to make sure I wasn't dehydrated.

Eventually I get through all of that hell and finally come back home. I make sure to shower/clean slowly as to not injure any of my incisions, I cut out all junk food so that I wouldn't throw up or make anything worse, and finish up the rest of my school projects to get all of that out of my mind.

After a few weeks I started to feel more normal and things started to get easier. I calmed back down and work was starting to slow down for a bit. Once the school semester was completely over I relaxed for a bit, played more games than I usually did and eventually started to plan for the upcoming summer.
