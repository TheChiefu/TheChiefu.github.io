Late 2025 Update

Written: 2025-09-05

Traveling

I guess my excitement wasn't satiated since after I came back from the concert I found another upcoming one. The Genshin 2024 tour was finishing up Janurary of 2025 in New York City, so I booked that and then went on to routing a 2 country trip to end off my year.

After doing some deal hunting I got good prices for planes tickets and hotels for both Japan and Australia. Only one month later I ended up landing in Osaka, Japan on November, 11th. I went to Himeji Castle, Kyoto, Otsu, Namba, and the two Nintendo offices (don't worry I asked the security if I could take a picture).

I spent so much time walking around I got blisters on my feet (again), but this time found some more local places I could visit more frequently for food and leisure if I wasn't up to traveling hours everyday. I did what I always do when abroad and try the American branches I'm used to and see how they differ. The Domino's Pizza was pretty good and fun to fill out the online form. I also got the teriyaki chicken sandwich from McDonalds again.

I also took a gander at the Universal Japan area before leaving to see what it looked like. I didn't book any tickets since I didn't think the cost would be worth it to go by myself. Either way I got some souvenirs for everyone at home:

  • Yoshi Plush (for my mom)
  • Hard Rock Cafe T-Shirt (for my dad)
  • Gundam Kit (from my friend Jerry)
  • Kit Kats (for my friend Jason)
  • Mini Uno Deck gachapon (for my friend Noah)
  • Genshin Keychains (for one of my online friends)
  • And lots and lots of Cinnamoroll plushes and merch (for my friend Nick)

I swear if my friend Nick comes to Japan, he'll be overwhelmed with how much things he'll want to buy. If it has a tiny body and big head, his bank account is getting drained.

Either way I jam everything into my carry on bag and make my way over to the KIX Airport for the next leg in my trip Australia. I get there early as normal and wait until I can get my ticket to get past the terminal gates. At first I was confused since my online tracker kept saying I had an error, despite having the correct credentials and passport information.

Come to find out, I didn't realize I needed to pay for a Visa for Australia and I had to talk to an assistant in order to get that stuff settled. Eventually I get all that hassle sorted out and hop on to the plane. As the plane is loaded up I get to my window seat on a 3 seater row. To my suprise no one sat next to me and I got the whole row to myself, to which I tried my hardest to get any semblance of sleep on.

Empty row on plane flight

A few hours go by and eventually land in Cairns, Australia with moderately okay sleep, still a bit groggy though. I try to go through customs as I normally would but for whatever reason get flagged. I walk to the side after being scanned and talked to one of the security agents. I explain who I am, how long I'll be there, and the usual. After some time they let me go and I go through normal security. When going back through security again to get to my terminal they take me aside and pull out a tiny shuriken keychain, they ask me if I want to get it checked, and I just tell them its easier to toss it out. By this point I'm just tired and want to be at my hotel.

After finally being done with security I notice my phone was a bit low on battery, casually I try to find a plug and completely forgot that the plugs were different. Thankfully I was in an airport and found a shop that sold converters. Finally, after waiting for a few hours during a layover I land in Sydney and walk about half an hour to my hotel. I open my door up, take a shower, and fall right on the bed to sleep.

The next day I make a plan to visit the opera house and see what options I have to get there. I saw I could take a train from the airport to the square right at the opera house. I get my stuff ready and realize I am completely packed for winter weather. I knew in my head that the northern hemisphere was in winter (it was November) but didn't pack accordingly for the southern hemisphere. Either way, I was there and wanted to make the best out of my trip so I headed out. It wasn't pleasant, but I went out in full pants and shirt in nice 35°C weather.

I walked around the area for a bit, took some panoramas, picked up a rock, and got some Australian branded Burger King and McDonalds (or I should say, Hungry Jack's & Maccas). I don't know why, but I thought people just called it "Maccas" as local slang, but the WiFi in the McDonalds actually said "Maccas" which I found endlessly ammusing. I also made sure to try out TimTams while I was there too (they took some time to adjust to).

TimTams and Smiths chips

Unfortunately my 2nd day was not as pleasant. I think because I was traveling so much I started to get sick. I took some local medicine but it for some reason made my hands started to have some circular blemishes appear, so I stopped taking it. It didn't hurt or anything but was worrying enough that I stopped. I ended up the last 2 days of my vacation there in my hotel watching some YouTube and talking to my friends at home. I still thought the trip was fun but at this point I was wanting to head back home (while endlessly sniffing).

The days went by, I packed up my bags, and took the long agonizing flights back from Australia to the US. As usual by the time I got home I took my shower and fell into bed. Scattering all my souvenirs on the bed. Closing my adventure in 2024 and sealing up my dream of visiting every continent (well, except for Antartica).
