Written: 2025-09-05

New York Concert

2025 Started and as usual I was going about my year as normal, but as the days went on I felt my excitement get higher as the date got closer for going to the Genshin concert. Initially I was going to go alone but my cousin overheard about my trip and was interested in going. At first I was a little shocked since its something that I didn't expect him to be interested in. He's not into the gaming community nor anime and as far as I know isn't too big into orchestra. But I think he just wanted to go somewhere with me and do something together.

Luckily this time when I went I didn't have to drive. There is a train station right in my town and it took us right into the station where the concert was adjacent to. It was a little boring traveling Amtrak for 3 hours, but it beats out driving and having to find New York City parking. Either way we get in the city pretty early; hang around eating some pizza and walk around until we start to see all the cosplayers appear. At first we weren't too exactly sure where the concert hall was, but once the colorful hairs started to pop up we knew we were in the right place.

As usual I was giddy, walking towards my seat and getting comfortable. I asked some cosplayers for some pictures and sent some of my friends pictures of their favorite characters. Again I got a pretty close view of the players. Eventually, the concert lights dim and the concert starts. The music starts playing and even though there was game scenes on the screens I tried to pay attention to the players.

After some time intermission comes up, I took a bathroom break and got a text from my cousin. He said the concert was great and asked me when I was heading back since he was already at the hotel. I then told him the concert wasn't over. Apparently he totally spaced out on what the intermission meant and left for the hotel. Either way I sat back down and listened to the rest of the concert.

Overall I'd say it was a solid 6/10, my main complaint was that the music wasn't like how it normally sounds in the game or soundtrack. I know its harder to do it live though, especially since they had to replace some more traditional instruments with different ones due to a lack of a local player who knows how to use it. I still enjoyed the concert though.

When I got back to the hotel I made sure to get in my bed quitely since my cousin was already asleep. The next morning we got on the early train and headed back home. Ending my journey of concerts and traveling over the last couple months (end of 2024 to start of 2025). Now I was back on my normal work schedule for a while.
